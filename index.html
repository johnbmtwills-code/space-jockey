SPACE JOCKEY

v0.3

Listen for the chime. Swing the couch onto the lit panel and fire before it winks out. Every panel you hit is gone for good: clear them all to win. The amber plating round the pillar is structure and can't be hit. Miss, or dither, and the sphere fires back.

On a computer the mouse or trackpad steers the couch directly (Esc releases it); click or Space to fire. On touch: drag to steer, tap to fire. Arrow keys also steer.
When the sphere returns fire, an amber zone lights up on the projectile's path. Aim at it and fire while the projectile is inside it. You have two interceptors per attack.
Headphones essential: the chime tells you where to look.
AirPods: turn off Spatialise Stereo in Control Centre while playing, or it fights the game's own positioning.
